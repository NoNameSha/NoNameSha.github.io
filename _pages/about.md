I am **Haichao Sha**, a Ph.D. student in Big Data Science and Engineering at **Renmin University of China**. I am currently a visiting student at the College of Computing and Data Science, **Nanyang Technological University**.

My research focuses on **differential privacy**, **DP optimization**, **privacy-preserving machine learning**, and **trustworthy large language models**. Recently, I have been working on differentially private optimization for large language models, including DP-SGD, private fine-tuning, zeroth-order DP optimization, privacy auditing, and privacy risks in MoE-based LLMs.

My recent research interests include:

- **Differentially Private Optimization:** designing utility-aware and theoretically grounded DP-SGD and DP fine-tuning methods.
- **Privacy-Preserving LLMs:** studying private training, fine-tuning, and auditing for large language models.
- **MoE Privacy and Expert Selection:** investigating privacy-aware expert selection and efficient DP adaptation for mixture-of-experts models.
- **Privacy Auditing:** developing canary-based and reasoning-based auditing methods for black-box LLMs.

I am always happy to discuss research ideas and potential collaborations.
