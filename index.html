About

I am Haichao Sha, a Ph.D. student in School of Information at Renmin University of China, under the supervision of Prof. Hong Chen and Prof. Yuncheng Wu.

My research focuses on differential privacy, DP optimization, and privacy-preserving machine learning. Recently I have been working on differentially private optimization for large language models.

My recent research interests include:

  • Privacy-preserving machine learning: theoretically grounded DP optimization and DP utility-aware methods.
  • Privacy of LLMs: private fine-tuning, inference, and auditing for large language models.

I am always happy to discuss research ideas and potential collaborations.
